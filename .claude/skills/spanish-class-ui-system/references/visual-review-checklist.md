# Visual Review Checklist

- Does the screen feel like Editorial Teaching Studio rather than a generic SaaS dashboard?
- Is the main action obvious within three seconds?
- Is at least 80% of the operational surface neutral?
- Are typography, spacing, and alignment coherent?
- Are cards used only where containment is meaningful?
- Are statuses recognizable without color?
- Are time and numbers tabular and scannable?
- Does mobile change composition rather than only shrink?
- Do long Serbian/Spanish labels fit or wrap intentionally?
- Are focus, loading, empty, error, and success states visually finished?
- Is motion restrained and informative?
- Are there any gradients, glows, arbitrary values, legacy tokens, or one-off patterns?
