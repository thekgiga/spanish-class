# Product Vision

Spanish Class is a calm teaching workspace centered on time, students, and lessons.

## Professor mental model

> I open my schedule, see what needs attention, and manage my teaching day.

## Student mental model

> I see my next lesson, choose a suitable available time, request it, and always know what happens next.

## Core product decisions

- One-to-one teaching is the primary experience.
- Professor approval is mandatory for every student-requested lesson.
- Professor-created lessons are confirmed immediately.
- The professor calendar is the operational home.
- The student booking experience is optimized for choosing, not administering, time.
- System concepts such as tokens, slot versions, worker jobs, and enums remain invisible.
