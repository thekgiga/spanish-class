ELITE EDUCATION — WEBSITE BRANDING GUIDE

FINAL LOGO RULE
The approved logo supplied by the client is the master artwork. Do not redraw, stretch,
recolour, crop into the logo, alter its EE monogram, alter the tilde, or change the
spacing inside the mark. Use the supplied master logo exactly as provided.

BRAND PALETTE
Burgundy #8B0E1A
Gold #D4AF7C
Light Beige #F9F6EF
Charcoal #1E1E24
White #FFFFFF

TYPOGRAPHY
Headlines: Playfair Display
Navigation/body: Montserrat
Fallbacks: Georgia / Arial

VISUAL LANGUAGE
Luxury editorial, warm Spanish architecture, education, travel, cultural immersion,
quiet confidence, premium guidance. Use generous whitespace, thin gold rules,
burgundy blocks, cream backgrounds, subtle paper texture and restrained line-art.

WEBSITE DIRECTION
Hero → Intro → Services → Study in Spain → Learn Spanish → Guidance → Spain experience →
Testimonials/Stories → CTA → Footer.

BUTTONS
Primary: Burgundy background / white text.
Secondary: Gold background / burgundy text.
Avoid gradients on buttons.

PHOTOGRAPHY
Prefer warm natural light, Spanish architecture, university/campus environments,
streets, books, language learning, travel details and authentic human moments.
Avoid generic corporate stock photography.

LOGO PLACEMENT
Use the exact final logo on clean white/cream areas. For dark sections, place the exact
logo on a white/cream card rather than altering the logo colours.
